# Paper Bird Journey - Interactive Narrative

An interactive web-based narrative where a folded paper bird embarks on a journey through life, with three distinct paths representing different life choices.

## Overview

Players guide a delicate paper bird to one of three destinations, each symbolizing a different philosophy:

- **Mountain (Freedom)** - The bird soars upward and disappears into sunlight
- **Ocean (Peace)** - The bird glides peacefully and lands on calm water
- **City (Connection)** - The bird transforms into a letter and lands on a window

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Click "Begin Journey" to start
3. Choose your path:
   - **Click the mountain peak** to send the bird toward freedom
   - **Drag the bird toward the waves** to seek peace
   - **Click the city skyline** to find connection

## Interactions

### Mountain Path
- **Action**: Click the mountain
- **Animation**: Bird flies upward, sunlight appears
- **Outcome**: Bird disappears into the light, symbolizing boundless freedom

### Ocean Path
- **Action**: Drag the bird horizontally toward the ocean
- **Animation**: Bird glides smoothly and lands on water
- **Outcome**: Bird floats peacefully on calm waves, embracing tranquility

### City Path
- **Action**: Click the city skyline
- **Animation**: Windows light up sequentially, bird transforms
- **Outcome**: Bird folds into a letter on a window sill, symbolizing human connection

## Features

- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (no dependencies)
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Responsive design
- Touch-friendly for mobile devices
- Drag-and-drop interaction for ocean path
- Visual feedback and hover effects
- Restart capability to explore different paths

## Technical Details

- **Drag interaction**: Uses mouse and touch events for ocean path
- **Click interactions**: Mountain and city respond to clicks
- **Animations**: CSS transitions and keyframe animations
- **Visual effects**: Dynamic sunlight, wave motion, lighting windows

## Symbolism

Each path represents a different life philosophy:

1. **Freedom (Mountain)**: Independence, aspiration, transcendence
2. **Peace (Ocean)**: Calm, acceptance, inner tranquility
3. **Connection (City)**: Community, communication, human bonds
